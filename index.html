Original animated shorts, from script to post

2D Animation Story Studio is the production and publishing tool behind 2D Animation Story, a channel of original "what if" stories told as short vertical 2D animations.

Every story is invented: an umbrella that only rains underneath itself, a shadow that leaves home without its owner, a snow globe with its own shop inside. The studio writes, animates and checks each short, then the creator decides whether and how it is published.

How it works

  1. 1. Create. The studio produces a 45–64 second vertical animation (1080×1920) with voice-over and captions.
  2. 2. Check. An automated quality check measures format, loudness and pacing. Videos that fail are fixed or discarded.
  3. 3. Approve. The creator watches the finished video in a private Telegram message and approves or rejects it. Nothing moves on without that approval.
  4. 4. Choose how to post. Before each TikTok post the creator sees the account name and chooses the privacy level (no default), whether to allow comments, duets and stitches, and whether the video is commercial content. The Music Usage Confirmation notice is shown before the Post button.
  5. 5. Publish. Only after the creator taps Post is the video sent to the creator's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, with the title and description the creator confirmed.
