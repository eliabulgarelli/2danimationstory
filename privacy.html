Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data 2D Animation Story Studio ("the studio", "we") uses when a creator connects a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account to it, and how that data is handled.

Data we access

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), account statistics (follower and like counts), the list of videos posted through the studio with their view counts, and the creator's posting options (allowed privacy levels, whether comments, duets and stitches are enabled, maximum video length). We upload videos only with the scopes the creator authorized.
  • YouTube: the channel ID and the view, like and comment counts of videos uploaded through the studio.
  • Instagram: the professional account ID and insights of media posted through the studio.

We do not access private messages, contacts, other people's content, or any data of viewers.

How we use it

  • To show the creator which account a video will be posted to and which posting options are available.
  • To publish videos the creator has approved, with the settings the creator chose.
  • To report view counts back to the creator so they can see which stories perform best.

We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the platforms themselves (TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram) when calling their APIs, and Telegram, which delivers the creator's private approval messages.

Storage and security

Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in a private code repository and are never written to files, logs or messages. Statistics are kept in that private repository and refreshed at least every 30 days. Data is kept only while the account stays connected.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • TikTok: open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, and remove the app.
  • YouTube: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove access.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app.

You can also email us and we will delete the stored tokens and statistics within 30 days.

Google and YouTube

The studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
